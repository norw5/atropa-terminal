# Method — Coverage (completeness by construction)

The reference core's coverage units are **mechanically defined**:

- every census child (dual-method census — blockscout internal-create index
  ∪ per-tx receipt pass, every row RPC-verified),
- every classified contract (minters, reserves, shared infrastructure, the
  Maria corpora, the addresses-sol named layer, the pinned bridge set),
- every pool of interest (both PulseX factories crawled end-to-end).

No editorial selection decides **what exists** in the core, so the core cannot
cherry-pick. Wherever selection *does* enter a page (examples, top-N tables,
"notable" callouts), the selection rule is stated on the page in the form
"rule @ block N" (e.g. "top 20 by WPLS-anchored liquidity @ block 27,435,924").

Community taxonomies ("t-share eco", curated spines, tracker lists) are
recorded as **aliases** on entity pages and in the Community-record layer;
they never define classes. Where they failed verification, the divergence is
recorded, not silently corrected.

The IRC corpus ships **complete and verbatim** with its own browser; any
curated excerpt of it lives only in the quarantine layer until the review
pipeline approves a reviewed selection.
